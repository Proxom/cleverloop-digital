How to make a contractor invoice or estimate

  1. Add your business details once. They are remembered in this browser for next time.
  2. List the work as line items. Labor, materials, rentals, disposal and permits on separate lines are easier for a customer to accept. Tick “Tax” only on lines that are taxable in your state.
  3. Price from cost and margin. Not sure what to charge? Use the job price calculator first.
  4. Estimate first, invoice later. Send the estimate with a deposit percentage. When the job is done, switch to Invoice, add any signed change orders and enter the deposit you received: the balance due updates.
  5. Save as PDF and send. Click Download PDF / Print and choose Save as PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is the invoice maker really free?

Yes. Create as many estimates and invoices as you like and save them as PDFs. No sign-up and no watermark across the page; a small Made with Clever Loop Digital line sits at the bottom of each document.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser. Nothing you type is sent to our server. Your business details and the current document are kept in this browser so they are still there next time; clear them any time with the Start over button.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Click Download PDF / Print, then choose Save as PDF as the printer. On a phone, use the share or print option of your browser.

Can I turn an estimate into an invoice?

Yes. Switch the document type from Estimate to Invoice: your line items stay, the number changes, and the deposit becomes a payment received so the invoice shows the balance due.

Does it work for any trade?

Yes. It is built for US home-service businesses such as handymen, painters, plumbers, electricians, landscapers, cleaners and remodelers, but any small business can use it.
