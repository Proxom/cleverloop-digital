Break-Even Calculator

How many jobs a month do you need just to cover your fixed costs? Enter your monthly overhead and the average numbers for one job. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is stored or sent.

How it works

Contribution per job = price − variable cost (materials, labor paid per job, fuel, fees). Break-even jobs = fixed costs ÷ contribution, rounded up. Example: $4,000 fixed ÷ ($900 − $500) = 10 jobs a month. Fixed costs include insurance, software, vehicle payments, rent and any salary you pay yourself.

Estimates for planning only. This is not accounting, tax or legal advice.
