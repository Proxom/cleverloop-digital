Deposit & Payment Schedule Calculator

Enter the job total, a deposit percentage and the number of progress payments. You get the deposit and a simple payment schedule. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is stored or sent.

How it works

Deposit = total × deposit%. Remaining balance = total − deposit, split equally across the progress payments. Example: $12,000 × 25% = $3,000 deposit; $9,000 split over 3 payments = $3,000 each. Deposit limits and payment-schedule rules differ by state and trade, so check your local rules before putting terms in a contract.
