How to Price Interior Painting

Price interior painting by the square foot of wall, ceiling and trim as separate lines, then check the total against your labor hours. Here are the published rates, what moves a quote, and a worked example.

Typical installed price for interior painting

These are consumer-facing figures from Angi's 2026 cost guides, not rules. They show where the market sits so you can tell whether your price is far above or below it.

What is pricedAngi 2026 range
Whole interior, per sq ft of floor area$2 – $6 (average $2.75 walls only, $4.70 with trim and ceilings)
Walls only (surface)$1 – $2 per sq ft
Smooth ceiling$1 – $3 per sq ft
Accent walls$2 – $6 per sq ft
Baseboard and trim$1 – $3 per linear ft (up to $6 if hard to reach)
Sanding and patching$0.50 – $0.75 per sq ft

By room, Angi lists a 10×10 ft room at $200 to $600, 12×12 ft at $290 to $870 and 16×16 ft at $515 to $1,540. By room type: bathroom $200 to $400, bedroom $300 to $750, kitchen $300 to $750, living room $900 to $2,000. The national average for a whole house is $2,022, with most jobs between $965 and $3,089, based on 65,117 projects reported by Angi pros.

What moves the price

  • Labor. Angi puts labor at 75 to 95 percent of a professional interior job (60 to 90 percent in its room guide). Hourly rates it cites are $20 to $50 for a painter and $20 to $40 for a handyman.
  • Ceiling height. A 10 ft ceiling adds about $1 per sq ft; cathedral ceilings above 12 ft need scaffolding and cost the most.
  • Coats and color change. Going light over dark needs extra coats. Angi puts each added coat at $50 to $70 per 300 to 400 sq ft of wall.
  • Prep and repairs. Drywall repair averages $600; wallpaper removal about $3 per sq ft; popcorn ceiling removal $1 to $2 per sq ft.
  • Paint. $15 to $40 per gallon in the house guide ($20 to $70 in the room guide), covering 300 to 400 sq ft. Primer is about $10 per quart.

Calculate paintable area and paint first

Wall area is the room perimeter times the ceiling height, minus doors and windows. A 14×12 ft room with 8 ft ceilings is 2 × (14 + 12) × 8 = 416 sq ft; subtract one door (20 sq ft) and two windows (15 sq ft each) for 366 sq ft. The ceiling is 14 × 12 = 168 sq ft. The painting estimate calculator does this for you and rounds up the gallons.

Set a minimum charge

A single closet or touch-up can cost more in driving, setup and cleanup than it earns. Set a minimum job charge from your hourly cost times the real time a small job takes. See how to calculate labor cost and your overhead rate for the inputs.

A worked quote

One 14×12 ft room, 8 ft ceilings, two coats, using example rates (yours will differ):

Walls: 366 sq ft × $2.25$823.50
Ceiling: 168 sq ft × $1.75$294.00
Paint: 4 gallons × $40.00$160.00
Supplies (tape, plastic, rollers)$75.00
Total$1,352.50

Check it against the market: Angi lists a 12×12 ft room at $290 to $870, so this is high for a bare room but includes the ceiling and a labor rate that must also cover overhead. If you are far above local quotes, check your rate before cutting it. These figures are examples, not recommended prices.

Common mistakes

  • Copying a national average instead of building the price from your own labor hours and overhead.
  • Quoting one lump sum, so the customer cannot see ceilings, trim and prep and negotiates the whole price.
  • Not stating how many coats are included and who supplies the paint.
  • Skipping prep, then absorbing patching and priming time.
  • Taking no deposit. Read how to write a contractor estimate for deposit wording.
