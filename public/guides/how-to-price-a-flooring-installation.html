How to Price a Flooring Installation

Price flooring per square foot of finished floor, with removal, underlayment, subfloor repair and trim as separate lines and waste added to the material. Here are the published rates, what moves a quote, and a worked example.

Typical installed price for flooring

These are consumer-facing figures from Angi and HomeGuide 2026 cost guides, not rules. They show where the market sits so you can tell whether your price is far above or below it.

The ranges are wide because material drives the total. Angi notes that small jobs can carry a higher rate per square foot because of minimum charges, and large jobs may get a discount.

What moves the price

  • Material. Sheet vinyl starts around $1 to $2 per sq ft and luxury vinyl plank reaches $12 per sq ft in material alone on Angi's figures. Hardwood and stone sit well above that, so quote the exact product.
  • What comes up. Angi puts carpet removal at about $1 to $1.50 per sq ft and tile removal at $2 to $7. Price removal as its own line by floor type.
  • The subfloor. Uneven concrete or damaged plywood needs repair or leveling before anything goes down. Angi lists subfloor replacement at $3 to $10 per sq ft. Put a unit price in the estimate before you start.
  • Layout. Diagonal patterns, many small rooms, stairs and transitions slow the crew and raise waste. Do not hide that time in a flat rate.
  • Furniture and haul-away. Moving furniture and disposing of old flooring are real costs. Angi lists disposal at $150 to $200 in its tile guide.

Calculate quantity with waste first

Add up the net area of every room, then add a waste allowance for cuts and mistakes. A 20×20 ft room is 400 sq ft; with 10 percent waste you order 440 sq ft of material but charge installation on the 400 sq ft that is laid. The flooring estimate calculator does the room math, waste and rounding for you.

Set a minimum charge

A closet or hallway can cost more in travel, setup and cutting than a per-square-foot price covers. Set a minimum job charge from your crew's hourly cost times the real time a small job takes. See how to calculate labor cost and your overhead rate for the inputs.

A worked quote

One 20×20 ft room (400 sq ft) of luxury vinyl plank over a clean subfloor, replacing carpet, using example rates (yours will differ):

Material: 440 sq ft (400 + 10% waste) × $3.25$1,430.00
Installation: 400 sq ft × $3.00$1,200.00
Carpet removal and haul-away: 400 sq ft × $1.25$500.00
Transitions and trim: 3 × $45$135.00
Total$3,265.00

Check it against the market: $3,265 over 400 sq ft is about $8.16 per sq ft installed, inside Angi's $2 to $22 range for vinyl and HomeGuide's $4 to $16 for luxury vinyl. The $1.25 removal rate is within Angi's carpet range of about $1 to $1.50. Subfloor repair is not included; quote it per square foot. These figures are examples, not recommended prices.

Common mistakes

  • Charging installation on the order quantity or forgetting to charge for the waste material.
  • Folding removal, underlayment and trim into one lump sum, so the customer cannot compare bids line by line.
  • Not stating the product, thickness and warranty in the estimate.
  • Leaving out a subfloor repair rate, then absorbing the cost of leveling.
  • Taking no deposit. Read how to write a contractor estimate for deposit wording.
