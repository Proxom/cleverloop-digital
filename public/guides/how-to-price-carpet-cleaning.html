How to Price Carpet Cleaning

Carpet cleaning is quoted per room for most homes and per square foot for large open areas and offices, with a minimum charge, stairs and stain or odor treatment on top. Here are the published rates, what moves a quote, and a worked example.

The sources overlap but do not match. Angi's per-room average is far wider than the per-room method prices, because it includes bigger rooms and specialty carpets (wool $100 – $400 per room in Angi's table). Compare several sources and your local competitors.

Per room, per square foot, or whole home

  • Per room. Fast to quote on the phone and the usual choice for homes. Say how large a room can be (the sources use roughly 200 to 250 sq ft) and whether closets, hallways and stairs count as rooms.
  • Per square foot. Better for open floor plans, oversized rooms and offices. Housecall Pro's example of a 2,000 sq ft quarterly contract uses $0.22 one-time and $0.18 recurring.
  • Whole-home package. A bundle of rooms at a lower per-room price. Angi lists $200 to $1,350 for small to medium homes.

What moves the price

  • Method. Bonnet and steam are the cheapest per room; dry cleaning, shampooing and encapsulation cost more.
  • Carpet material. Angi lists nylon at $60 to $300 per room and wool at $100 to $400.
  • Soil level, pets and stains. Traffic lanes and pet accidents need pre-treatment and sometimes a second pass.
  • Access. Stairs, furniture that has to be moved and long carries add labor.
  • Job size. Small jobs fall under the minimum charge. Bigger jobs often get a lower rate per square foot.
  • Market. Urban and high-cost areas run higher.

Check the price against your costs

Estimate on-site time plus drive and setup time, multiply by your hourly cost, then add solution, fuel, equipment wear, insurance and overhead. Setup and drive time are the same for one room or five, which is why a minimum charge exists. A worked quote

A steam cleaning job for four bedrooms and a staircase, using example rates (yours will differ). Rooms at $50 each is 4 × $50 = $200.00. Stairs of 13 steps at $3 is 13 × $3 = $39.00. Pet odor treatment in one room at $30 is $30.00.

Steam cleaning, 4 rooms × $50$200.00
Stairs, 13 steps × $3$39.00
Pet odor treatment, 1 room × $30$30.00
Total$269.00

Check it against the market: $50 per room sits inside Angi's $40 to $90 steam range, $3 per step sits inside the $2 to $5 range, $30 sits inside the $20 to $40 odor range, and the total is above the $75 to $125 minimum charge. Housecall Pro's own example is similar: 5 rooms at $55 plus pet odor in 2 rooms at $30 comes to $335. These figures are examples, not recommended prices.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping a minimum charge, so a one-room job loses money after drive and setup time.
  • Not defining what counts as a room, then arguing over a 400 sq ft living room.
  • Giving stairs, stain treatment and furniture moving away instead of listing them.
  • Skipping a written estimate. Read how to write a contractor estimate for what to include.
