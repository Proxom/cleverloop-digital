The margin trap

A 25% markup is not a 25% margin

Margin is the share of the price you keep. Markup is added to cost. Mix them up and every quote leaves money behind. On a $700 job the gap is $58.33, and it grows with every job you win.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Are the calculators really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no paywall. Every calculator runs in your browser; nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is profit as a share of cost; margin is profit as a share of price. A 25% markup on a $700 job gives $875, which is only a 20% margin. For a true 25% margin you divide by 0.75 and quote $933.33.

Who are these tools for?

Small home-service businesses in the US: plumbers, electricians, painters, roofers, handymen, cleaners, landscapers and similar trades.

Do you use cookies or track me?

No. The site sets no cookies and loads no trackers or third-party scripts. See the privacy notice.

Is this financial or tax advice?

No. The tools give planning estimates. Check local taxes, licensing rules and your own books before you rely on a number.
