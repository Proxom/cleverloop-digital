Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Controller: Erkan Bedir, trading as Clever Loop Digital, United Kingdom. Contact: hello@cleverloopdigital.com.

What we collect

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. The calculators run in your browser; the numbers you enter are not sent to us. Our hosting provider (Hostinger) keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser and pages requested, for security and operation.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply. Legal basis (UK GDPR): our legitimate interest in answering enquiries. We keep correspondence as long as needed for the enquiry and normal record keeping.

Purchases and mailing lists

No sales or sign-up forms exist yet. Before any are added, this page will say which provider handles payment or email, what is collected and why. Payments will be handled by the payment provider; we will not see or store card numbers. We will only send marketing email to people who sign up for it, with an unsubscribe link in every message.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, and where applicable EU GDPR and US state laws such as the CCPA, you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use. We do not sell personal data. Email hello@cleverloopdigital.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
