What the sheet calculates

  • Quote price before and after sales tax on materials
  • Profit on the job, markup on cost and profit per labor hour
  • Deposit due at your chosen percentage
  • What your margin becomes if the client negotiates 10% or 20% off, and your break-even price

Yellow input cells, everything else is formulas. No macros, no add-ons, no subscription.

Plus five bonus tabs in the same workbook

  • Hourly Rate: the rate to charge for your income goal after overhead and unbillable time
  • Change Order: price extra work as labor + disruption allowance + marked-up materials
  • Job Profit Log: list finished jobs and see the margin you really made on each, and in total
  • Customer Estimate: a clean estimate to send, with line items, tax, deposit and a signature line
  • Customer Invoice: bill the finished job with change orders, subtract the deposit and show the balance due
