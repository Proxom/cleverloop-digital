Contractor Quote & Margin Sheet

Launching soon · planned price $9

One Google Sheets page for home-service pros (plumbers, electricians, roofers, painters and similar). Enter materials, labor hours, labor rate, subcontractor costs, overhead and the margin you want, and get the quote price, your profit and the deposit to ask for.

What the sheet calculates

  • Quote price before and after sales tax on materials
  • Profit on the job, markup on cost and profit per labor hour
  • Deposit due at your chosen percentage
  • What your margin becomes if the client negotiates 10% off, and your break-even price

Yellow input cells, everything else is formulas. No macros, no add-ons, no subscription.

Prefer to try the math first? Use the free job price calculator or see all free tools.

How to get it

The checkout is not open yet. To be told when it is, email hello@cleverloopdigital.com with the subject “Quote sheet”. We will only write to you about this sheet.

Figures are estimates. Margin means profit divided by price; markup means profit divided by cost. This is a tool, not accounting, tax or legal advice.
