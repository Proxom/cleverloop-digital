# Forum answer taslakları (Erkan göndermez/yazmaz; sadece hazır metin)
Kural: önce gerçekten yardımcı ol, bağlantıyı yalnızca soru fiyatlandırma/hesaplama ise ekle; kuralları (self-promo) oku. Hiçbir şey paylaşılmadı.

## 1. "How do I price a job / what markup should I use?" (esnaf toplulukları)
Markup and margin are not the same. If your cost is $700 and you want a 25% margin, the price is cost / (1 - 0.25) = $933.33, not $700 x 1.25 = $875. The gap is real money on every job. Quick check: margin = (price - cost) / price. A free no-signup calculator that does the conversion: https://cleverloopdigital.com/tools/markup-margin-calculator.html

## 2. "What should my hourly rate be?"
Start from the income you need, add overhead and unbillable time, then divide by billable hours only. Many solo operators bill 50-60% of their paid hours. Calculator that shows the arithmetic: https://cleverloopdigital.com/tools/hourly-rate-calculator.html

## 3. "How many jobs a month to break even?"
Fixed costs / (average job price - variable cost per job). Example: $4,000 / ($900 - $500) = 10 jobs. https://cleverloopdigital.com/tools/break-even-calculator.html
