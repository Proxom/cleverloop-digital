### Patterns worth copying (all allowed under our operating model)
1. **Own a buyer's search term** with a product page that *is* the answer (HeadshotPro, Tally templates, Revid tools). Postma: "I only build products where there's SEO."
2. **Free product that distributes itself**: Tally's free forms carry a badge seen by every form respondent. Our equivalent: documents a contractor sends to homeowners.
3. **Anchor against the expensive alternative**: HeadshotPro's "studio $300-800 vs $29" table; TypingMind's "$39 once vs $240/yr".
4. **One-time pricing sells well for tools** (TypingMind, ShipFast, HeadshotPro); subscriptions where value is ongoing.
5. **Page anatomy**: one-line promise; the product/preview above the fold; a counter of real usage (only once real); comparison table; money-back guarantee; FAQ; many long-tail pages (templates, tools, comparisons).

### What NOT to copy
- Audience-driven launches (X/LinkedIn/Product Hunt): forbidden by our model.
- Fake scarcity ("7 left", countdown timers): dishonest, against our rules.
- Usage counters or testimonials before we have real ones.
